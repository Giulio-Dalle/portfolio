Grandma, your lentils are amazing!

Grandma, your lentils are amazing! is an illustrated cookbook developed by reworking an existing fairy tale.

The project is a 16-page booklet aimed at children, designed to encourage and educate them to eat vegetables through fun and dynamic graphics. The project had two main constraints: using only one typeface and including an illustrated element on every page.

In addition to the cookbook, a short animated video was created in which the stories and characters from the book come to life, turning the editorial product into a cross-media project.


Credits: T. Patrini, R. Santarelli, P. Savarese, C. Sportoletti.
