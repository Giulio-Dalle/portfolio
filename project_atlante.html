From Map to the World

From Map to the World is a personal project born from the combination of two of my greatest passions: geography and photography.

Inspired by the work of Luigi Ghirri, the project explores the relationship between imagination and reality, between the desire to set off and the concrete experience of travel. The goal is to collect and share some of my travel-themed photographs — those imagined in front of an atlas, dreaming of distant destinations, and those captured in real life during my journeys around the world.

All images were taken with my iPhone, aiming to show the world through a tool that everyone has at hand: simple, immediate, and always with us.
