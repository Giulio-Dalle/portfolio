Ciao!

I’m Giulio, I’m 22 years old, and I’m a graphic designer with a strong passion for visual communication. After discovering my interest in graphic design during high school, I decided to enroll at the Politecnico di Milano, where I graduated in Communication Design.

During my university studies, I challenged myself across various areas of design, experimenting with different languages and techniques. I also had the opportunity to spend a semester at the Vilnius Academy of Arts, where I was exposed to an international and experimental approach to visual design.

Today, I work as a graphic designer at SBS S.p.A., where I focus on visual communication and packaging design. I’m a curious and detail-oriented person, always looking for new ways to communicate ideas clearly and meaningfully.
