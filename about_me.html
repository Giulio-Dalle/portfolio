Ciao!

I’m Giulio, a 23-year-old Italian graphic designer with a strong passion for visual communication, a curious mindset, and a sharp attention to detail. I’m always looking for meaningful and effective ways to turn ideas into visual experiences.

After discovering my interest in graphic design during high school, I decided to study Communication Design at Politecnico di Milano, where I explored different areas of design, constantly challenging myself through new languages, tools, and creative approaches. During my studies, I also spent a semester at Vilnius Academy of Arts, experiencing a more experimental and international perspective on visual design.

Alongside design, sports have always played an important role in my life. I played basketball for many years, reaching high levels, an experience that helped me develop discipline, teamwork, consistency, and a strong goal-oriented mindset — qualities that still shape the way I approach design today.

I currently work as a graphic designer at SBS S.p.A., focusing on visual communication, graphic design, pruduct and packaging design across both digital and physical channels. I’m constantly driven by the desire to grow, experiment, and create work that is both visually engaging and meaningful.
