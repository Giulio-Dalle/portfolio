Eyes on the Prize!

Eyes on the Prize! is a 64-page volume designed to function both as an illustrated book and a poster. The project examines the relationship between money and typography, focusing on how prices are displayed in supermarkets.

The layout is inspired by the visual organization of large supermarkets, guiding the reader through aisles of products and showing how typography is used to influence the shopping experience.

The contemporary analysis is complemented by a selection of coupons from the 1950s, highlighting the evolution of visual pricing strategies over the years.

The volume is unbound: when laid out side by side, the pages form a large poster composed of coupons and discount vouchers. On the opposite side, the research focuses on the typography used today in stores, creating a visual dialogue between past and present.
